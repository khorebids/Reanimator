# Booty / Side Ass: manual fan experiment

Boob is displayed as Booty. Single and Full body remain unchanged. Side Ass now builds a manual quarter fan; it does not add automatic displacement, springs or contact compression.

1. Select the leg sprite and upper leg bone.
2. Click Draw outer arc and draw only the outer quarter arc, not a closed region. The endpoints define the two orthogonal axes. The first axis points opposite the leg (Flip back axis reverses it); the second points toward the other arc endpoint.
3. Apply Side Ass. Arc points defaults to 12. Two inner rows at one-third and two-thirds radius connect to a selected-leg-weighted center. Local geometry inside the fan is rebuilt; vertices, UVs and weights outside the region are retained, except stale generated helper influences being repaired during migration/redrawing.
4. Move the two helpers along their axes in the bone editor. Save/apply the PSD and Cook Prefab to continue in the scene.

Both helpers are independent children of the selected upper leg and inherit its rotation. `<leg>_booty` controls the back axis and `<leg>_booty_press` controls the perpendicular axis; neither drives the other. Both helpers start at the upper leg bone origin. Their translation-to-deformation weights remain unchanged; initial handle position does not affect translation-only skinning. No SideAssRig2D is attached for manual fans, and they are not marked as automated bones.

At angle theta on the outer arc: back weight = 0.5 cos(theta), perpendicular weight = 0.5 sin(theta), selected upper leg weight = the remainder. Inner rows multiply both helper weights by squared normalized radius; the center is fully weighted to the selected upper leg. Equal radii and equal axial helper translations preserve a circular outer arc; unequal translations give an ellipse. This relation assumes translations along the helper axes, not rotations.

The quarter fan uses neighboring radial rows and angular sectors rather than long triangles spanning the whole selected region. The local patch is joined to preserved outside topology with constrained tessellation and split boundary intersections. Recipe metadata under Library/Reanimator/SideAss records that the helpers are manual, including for standalone/repeated prefab cooking.

All traversed contour points and exact segment intersections are retained. This draws the arc only; Apply Side Ass remains a separate step. Single-curve revision: Full arc — helper influence replaces the two previous blend curves. Time runs by contour length from the very start of the attachment extension to the final arc endpoint. Time [0, 0.5) belongs to the back helper; [0.5, 1] to the perpendicular helper. Curve height is the helper weight on the contour (clamped to 0–1); inward it is multiplied by squared radial falloff. The selected leg gets the remainder. No independent angular mix or cap envelope modifies the curve. The default has two 0.5-height lobes and zero at start, midpoint and end. Preview and Apply use the same evaluator.

Helper smoothing is a persistent 0–1 slider, default 0. It interpolates between the hard half split and a linear crossfade over curve time 0.25–0.75. At full smoothing the midpoint shares helper influence equally. Curve height, radial falloff and residual leg weight remain unchanged, and times outside that interval retain their original helper. Changes update the posed preview immediately and apply through the same weight evaluator.

Quick — auto arc + apply builds the automatic quarter-leg contour and immediately applies Side Ass using the current influence curve, helper smoothing, arc point count and flip. The authored curve is preserved. Successful Quick exits the temporary preview; test helper offsets and test leg rotation are not baked. Repeated Quick is checked for stable geometry and no duplicate helpers.

Butt scene control: Reani IK automatically finds sibling manual helpers named <upper-leg>_booty (parallel/back) and <upper-leg>_booty_press (perpendicular), both directly parented to that upper leg. This works on existing cooked prefabs as well as new ones; sprite renderer nodes and incomplete/cross-parent pairs are ignored. The floating green Butt circle rotates both helper bones by the same angle, preserving their origins. Its square moves the parallel helper along its own axis on vertical drag and the perpendicular helper along its own axis on horizontal drag. Both support Undo and prefab instance overrides; Show Butt Control toggles visibility. No procedural runtime posing or spring is attached.

SC child revision: each controlled helper owns a same-length child named <helper>_SC. The child coincides with its parent in world position and rotation (zero local offset and identity local rotation). All skin influences move to the child; the parent stays weightless and remains the Butt control target. Existing weight values and bind matrices are preserved during migration, across every cached sprite layer. Curve edits and regenerated fans target the SC children. Cook and ReaniIK discover them in the existing Soft Contour spring group; parent helpers are excluded. Existing manual geometry can be upgraded without rebuilding its mesh.

Direction-aware Butt square: dragging is now resolved against both helpers' current world axes, rather than fixed vertical/parallel and horizontal/perpendicular roles. A quarter turn swaps which bone responds to horizontal or vertical motion; oblique directions share the drag continuously, including mirrored and nonuniformly scaled parents. Each helper still travels only along its own axis. The square displays the combined helper displacement, so rotating the circle does not make its thumb jump.

Cook visibility: all generated skeleton bone GameObjects are inactive by default, hiding Scene bone gizmos. Sprite renderers, rig targets and the ReaniIK root remain active. SpriteSkin uses the referenced inactive transforms; ReaniIK explicitly evaluates enabled elbow/shoulder/legacy Side Ass drivers on inactive bones so automatic deformation continues. Scene manipulation is through the floating rig controls.

ALLIN Side Ass: Full PSD / Skeletron exposes independent Left Leg and Right Leg switches, persisted per PSD alongside its Side Ass recipes. Selected l_leg / r_leg sprites use Quick with the current global influence curve, helper smoothing, point count and flip after SC. Reanimate applies Side Ass after the manually drawn SC masks and before PSD Apply / Cook. Unselected legs receive no Side Ass operation.
